# Future Go-to-Market Sprints

## Sprint 1 — Pilot the report with one VC team

- **Goal:** Check whether the report saves time and helps analysts prepare for a first meeting.
- **Audience:** One VC team that screens early-stage B2B startups.
- **Channel:** Ask for a warm introduction through a professional contact.
- **Deliverable:** A two-week pilot. One analyst reviews five startup reports, checks the sources, and records what was useful or missing.
- **Success metric:** Reduce median analyst time by at least 25% across five cases, compared with the team's usual process for similar cases. Include time spent checking sources and correcting the report. The analyst should rate at least four of the five reports as useful for initial screening.

## Sprint 2 — Reach three more VC teams through accelerator referrals

- **Goal:** Test whether accelerator referrals can bring new VC teams into a pilot.
- **Audience:** Three early-stage VC teams. Accelerator managers are the referral partners.
- **Channel:** Ask two accelerator managers to introduce the project to funds in their networks.
- **Deliverable:** A short demo, one sample report, and a pilot guide for each interested team.
- **Success metric:** Hold demo calls with three new VC teams. At least two teams should agree to try the tool, and at least one should review three startup reports during the pilot.
